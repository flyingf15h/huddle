How it works

From one prompt to merged code, with four agents working at once

01

Prompt your own agent, like always

Ask for something big the normal way. Your agent sees it's more than one person's work and that teammates' agents are free.

02

It delegates on its own

Your agent plans the work and hands parts to teammates' agents, picking whoever has the most usage left, so nobody hits their limit halfway.

03

Agents build in parallel

Every file an agent touches is locked until it's done, and the others work around it. When one needs to know something, it asks the agent that knows.

04

Checked, then merged

A second agent reviews each change and your tests run before anything lands. Broken work waits on its own branch instead of breaking main.

Why huddle

Other tools run more agents. huddle gets them working together.

Agent managers let one person run several agents. Vendor tools work for their own agent. huddle connects every teammate's agent, whichever one they use.

01

Across people, not just tabs

Coordinates agents on everyone's laptop, live, before anything is committed or pushed.

02

Any agent, one board

Claude Code, Codex and Cursor share the same locks, tasks, rules and costs. Nobody has to switch tools.

03

Your own plans

Agents run on each person's existing subscription and computer. huddle adds the teamwork, not another AI bill.

Safe to leave running overnight

  • Rules every agent followsCommands that are always fine, and ones no agent may ever run, like pushing or deploying.
  • Secrets stay on your laptop.env files and keys are never uploaded, and tokens are stripped from everything shared.
  • Your conversation stays yoursTeammates see what your agent did, not what you said to it, unless you choose to share it.

FAQ

Questions

Whose AI subscription does it use?

Everyone's own. Each person keeps their Claude, Codex or Cursor plan on their own computer. huddle connects the agents, not the logins, so there's no extra AI bill.

Can our team mix Claude Code, Codex and Cursor?

Yes. Locks, live edits, messages, tasks and usage work the same for all three, and the dashboard shows who uses which.

Does it change our repository?

No. huddle keeps its settings outside git, so joining doesn't add files your teammates have to merge, and leaving takes one command.

What do teammates see?

Which files your agent is editing, the tools it runs, the tasks it's on and messages sent to it. Your conversation with your agent stays private unless you turn on full sharing, and private files like .env never leave your computer.

How many people is it for?

It's built for small teams of two to about ten people who each code with an AI agent, like a class project, a side project with friends or an early startup.
