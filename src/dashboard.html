How huddle works
Everyone's Claude works on the same project at the same time, each from their own computer, without overwriting each other.

  
    
    
  • See what everyone is doingThe team list shows what each person and their Claude are working on. Every Claude is told when someone starts something new.
    • 
    
  • No overwritesA file a Claude is editing is locked for everyone else. Whole folders can be locked for bigger changes.
    • 
    
  • Hand off workSend a request to the team, and one Claude splits it into tasks, or send it to one person's Claude. Each runs as its own Claude in the background.
    • 
    
  • Track progressTasks show who owns what and how far along it is.
    • 
    
  • Shared brief and memoryEvery Claude reads the project brief, team memory and skills when it starts.
    • 
    
  • Code and hand-offsBrowse everyone's shared code, watch edits live, and see where each session left off.
    • 
  

  

    What your Claude does without you
    

      
ActiveRuns requests and answers messages sent to your Claude, and plans and takes team tasks. The default.

      
Requests onlyRuns requests and answers messages sent to your Claude, from people or other Claudes. Leaves team tasks to others.

      
OffNothing runs in the background. Requests and messages wait until you open Claude in the project.

    

    Requests never need approval. Commands outside the allowed list always do, in every mode. Change the mode from the menu at the bottom of the sidebar.
