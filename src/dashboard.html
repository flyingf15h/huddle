How huddle works

  

    
    
      
  1. The cards at the top are the people on the team. Each says what that person and their Claude are doing right now. Click one to see only them.
    2. 
      
  2. Timeline shows what happens, grouped by person: what they asked their Claude, what it did, and what it said. Hover a reply to read all of it.
    3. 
      
  3. Message Claude gives the Claudes a job. Full team: one Claude splits it into tasks they share. Pick a person to give it to just their Claude. A card that says Takes requests or Autopilot starts right away.
    4. 
      
  4. Tasks on the right shows progress, and which files are being edited. A file someone is editing is locked for everyone else until they're done.
    5. 
      
  5. My Claude (top right) is your own setting: takes requests runs what teammates send you while your Claude is open, full autopilot also takes team-wide work, off makes everything wait for you.
    6. 
    

    
Code shows each person's shared branch file by file, with live edits. Brief & memory is what every Claude reads when it starts.
