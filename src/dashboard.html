How huddle works

  

    
    
      
  1. The cards at the top are the people on the team. Each one says what that person and their Claude are doing right now. Click a card to see only them.
    2. 
      
  2. The timeline shows everything as it happens: messages, what each Claude was asked, what it ran and edited, and what it replied.
    3. 
      
  3. Message Claude (bottom) gives the Claudes a job. Pick Full team and one Claude splits it into tasks they share, or pick a person to give it to just their Claude. A card that says Takes requests or Autopilot means that Claude starts right away.
    4. 
      
  4. My Claude (top right) is your own setting: takes requests (default) runs what teammates send you while your Claude is open, full autopilot also takes team-wide work, off makes everything wait for you.
    5. 
      
  5. On the right: task progress, and which files are being edited. A file someone is editing is locked for everyone else until they're done. Click any file to see it live.
    6. 
    

    
Code shows each person's shared branch file by file, and live edits as they happen. Brief & memory is what every Claude reads when it starts. Invite people shows the exact steps to send a teammate.
