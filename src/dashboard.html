How huddle works

  

    
    
      
  1. The cards at the top are the people on the team. Each one says what that person and their Claude are doing right now. Click a card to see only them.
    2. 
      
  2. The timeline shows everything as it happens: messages, what each Claude was asked, what it ran and edited, and what it replied.
    3. 
      
  3. The box at the bottom sends a message to the team, or (switch to Ask the Claudes) gives the Claudes a job. One of them splits it into tasks and they share the work.
    4. 
      
  4. On the right: task progress, and which files are being edited. A file someone is editing is locked for everyone else until they're done. Click any file to see it live.
    5. 
    

    
Team knowledge (the project brief, shared notes and skills) is what every Claude reads when it starts. Invite people shows the exact steps to send a teammate.
